# Image sources register

No article image is used yet. **Every published image needs a complete entry here before it is added to the website**, and no image is added before the owner has seen the candidate and its licence evidence (DESIGN-SPEC.md 9.3, 12.2).

## Rules
- Use only images whose source page clearly documents commercial-use permission, with the right to crop, resize and convert. Acceptable: public domain / CC0, or a licence that explicitly allows commercial use and modification (with attribution where the licence requires it).
- Not acceptable: non-commercial or no-derivatives licences, editorial-use-only images, images without a stated licence, watermarked images, and images showing names of real people, fabricated credentials or logos, or staged clinical scenes that could suggest medical endorsement.
- Prefer neutral subjects (food, water, measuring tape, abstract shapes). Avoid identifiable real people unless a commercial model release is stated.
- Never hot-link an image. Images are local files in `src/assets/journal/`.
