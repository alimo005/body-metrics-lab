clinical_notes Clinical & Physiological Context

Energy conversion utilizes standard Atwater general factors (4 kcal/g for protein and carbohydrate, 9 kcal/g for lipid structures). The Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Ranges (AMDR) established by the Institute of Medicine advise 10–35% protein, 45–65% carbohydrate, and 20–35% dietary fat for adults to mitigate chronic disease vulnerability while maintaining metabolic homeostasis.

General Energy Conversion Equations

Macronutrient mass calculations employ the classic Atwater system, accounting for heat of combustion adjusted for human gastrointestinal bioavailability:

Protein (g) = (Daily kcal × % Protein ÷ 100) ÷ 4 kcal/g
Carbohydrate (g) = (Daily kcal × % Carb ÷ 100) ÷ 4 kcal/g
Fat (g) = (Daily kcal × % Fat ÷ 100) ÷ 9 kcal/g
Assumes complete digestion • Pure lipid substrate calculated at 9.0 kcal/g
science

AMDR Evidence Framework

Co-established by the National Academy of Medicine and USDA, Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Ranges ensure adequate consumption of essential micronutrient carriers:

  • Protein: Supplies essential amino acids for lean skeletal synthesis, hormone synthesis, and structural repair.
  • Carbohydrate: Sustains central nervous system glucose obligates and replenishes intrahepatic and muscular glycogen reserves.
  • Lipids: Facilitates lipophilic micronutrient transport (Vitamins A, D, E, K) and endocrine steroidogenesis.
