Standard Calculation Formula

Validated WHO Reference Matrix
International Standard (SI) Metric
BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²

Weight measured in kilograms divided by the square of stature measured in meters.

US Customary System Imperial
BMI = 703 × weight (lbs) / [height (in)]²

Weight measured in pounds multiplied by 703 conversion factor, divided by height squared in inches.

Calculations adhere to standard adult anthropometric cut-offs established by the CDC and WHO. Strictly applies to non-pregnant adults aged 20 and older.
