medical_services Clinical & Physiological Context

Resting metabolic rate accounts for the energy required for basic cellular maintenance, respiration, and autonomic function at rest. TDEE reflects this baseline scaled by your selected physical activity factor. Note: Predictive equations provide statistical population estimates; true metabolic rates vary based on thyroid function, body composition, and genetics.

Simplified Mifflin-St Jeor (1990)

Widely accepted as the most accurate non-clinical predictive equation for estimating Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR) in healthy adult populations without dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA).

Male Equation RMR = (10 × wt kg) + (6.25 × ht cm) - (5 × age) + 5
Female Equation RMR = (10 × wt kg) + (6.25 × ht cm) - (5 × age) - 161
Mifflin MD et al., Am J Clin Nutr 1990 arrow_outward

Total Daily Energy (TDEE)

TDEE incorporates metabolic baseline expenditure compounded with non-exercise activity thermogenesis (NEAT), exercise activity, and food thermogenesis via standardized multiplier factors.

Standard Formulation TDEE = Estimated RMR × Physical Activity Level (PAL)

Activity coefficients derived from empirical doubly labelled water (DLW) validations established under the joint FAO/WHO/UNU expert consultations on human energy requirements.

FAO/WHO Human Energy Requirements Report arrow_outward
